Data & definitions

Transparent, auditable assumptions
Completed shift value

Sum of hours × hourly rate for completed shifts. A gross transaction-value proxy, not company revenue.

Fill rate

Filled requests ÷ all shift requests. Filled includes completed and subsequently canceled shifts.

Cancellation rate

Canceled shifts ÷ filled shifts.

Adoption

Fictional partner-level adoption score; an operational metric placeholder until a real denominator is defined.
